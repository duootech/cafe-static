The Real Cost of Old-School Cafe Operations

Running a cafe is hard work. Doing it manually makes it twice as slow and prone to costly mistakes. Here is how modern software makes the difference.

Paper Tickets & Lost Orders

Written tickets get greasy, lost, or misread, causing kitchen delays, food waste, and frustrated customers waiting for their drinks.

The Solution: Real-time POS display. Orders go straight from the screen to the barista queue instantly, color-coded by wait time.

Staff Scheduling Chaos

Managing shifts via text messages and paper spreadsheets leads to double-scheduling, forgotten shifts, and frantic calling during peak rushes.

The Solution: Smart visual calendar scheduler. Set recurring patterns, avoid conflicts, and allow baristas to claim open shifts with one click.

Inventory Guesswork

Not knowing ingredient levels results in running out of oat milk mid-rush, or over-ordering fresh pastries that end up thrown away.

The Solution: Auto-deducting stock count. Every latte sold automatically subtracts coffee beans and milk, sending push alerts for low stock.
System Capabilities

Designed Specifically for Cafe Workflows

Every element of Cafefy is tailored to cafe operations, eliminating friction points so your team can focus on crafting the perfect cup.

Fast POS Terminal

High-speed order taking designed for iPad or touchscreen. Add modifiers (extra shot, oat milk, iced) in a single tap.

Role-Based Access

Limit access by role. Baristas see POS, Managers handle cash drops, and Admins view high-level profit metrics.

Dynamic Inventory Tracker

Track cups, beans, syrups, and milk down to the milliliter. Automatically flags items nearing critical thresholds.

Visual Shift Scheduler

Plan weekly grids in minutes. Send notifications to employee mobile devices immediately when schedules publish.

Real-Time Reports

Identify peak hour sales, average transaction size, staff performance metrics, and ingredient profit margins instantly.

Table Layout & Floor Planner

Custom map representing your tables. Track occupied, reserved, and dirty tables in real-time from a central view.

How It Works
